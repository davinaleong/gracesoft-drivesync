# Asset Mapping

## Wordmarks

Ideal for the brand components of navbars.

- `wm.png/svg`: Full company and product name in brand colours.
- `wm-b.png/svg`: Full company and product name in black.
- `wm-w.png/svg`: Full company and product name in white.

## Logo

Ideal for avatars and favicons.

- `logo.png/svg`: Initials of the product name in brand colours.
- `logo-b.png/svg`: Initials of the product name in black.
- `logo-w.png/svg`: Initials of the product name in white.
- `logo-square.png`: Initials of the product name in brand colours in a 1:1 canvas. Mainly for site icon.
